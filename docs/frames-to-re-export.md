# Frames still needing a higher-resolution export

**71 of the 112 frames** on the showroom panel have not been upgraded — 66 unique files, since a few frames are used twice (hero and attract).

For every one of these the Drive holds a low-res preview and a master and nothing in between, and every master is over the ~6 MB the connector will transfer. Sixty-five are 1600px on the long edge; the six Birre Villa frames are 2048px, which is 112px short of the threshold where a portrait hero takes the whole screen.

## What to export

The exact files listed below, at:

- **~2560px on the short edge** (the panel is 2160×3840, so this leaves headroom)
- **JPEG, quality 80**
- **loose files, not zipped** — a zip cannot be read through the connector
- **under 6 MB each** — measured ceiling is between 6.24 and 6.61 MB

Into the shared **"05 - Project images"** folder. One subfolder per project is tidier but not required.

**Keep the filenames exactly as they are.** That is how each frame is matched back to the slot it fills on screen.

  other two frames already came through at 3500×1969.
- **Lisbon Pied-à-Terre** frame `(3)` already sits at 5.83 MB and is readable, but
  it is not one of the six frames chosen for that project. If you would rather swap
  a frame than re-export, that one is free.

  export would tip its portrait heroes into the full-screen treatment.
- **Craft has no photography at all.** Its five stages — the Cascais atelier, the
  workshops, the Port of Lisbon warehouse, crates in transit, an installation on
  site — do not exist anywhere in the archive. That is a shoot, not an export, and
  it is the one section the screen cannot currently make.
