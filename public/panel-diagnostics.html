Panel diagnostics

Phase 0. Run this on the panel, in Fully Kiosk, in the mounted orientation, before a single screen is laid out. Fill in §1 of the brief from what it reports — nothing downstream is allowed to be designed against an assumption this page can settle.

Viewport and density

Touch

Put five fingers on the pad, then two, then drag one slowly. IR panels report no contact radius and no pressure, register a touch a few millimetres before the glass, and go jittery on multi-touch. PCAP reports real radii. This is the answer that decides pinch vs double-tap and 96px vs 120px targets.

Frame rate

Under a deliberately expensive composite. Anything below 58 is the SoC telling you something.

Image decode — AVIF vs WebP

Same 2160×3840 source in both formats. AVIF is smaller on the wire and more expensive to decode; on a weak SoC a larger WebP can render faster. Do not assume — pick the one that holds 60fps on this panel, even if the files are bigger.
